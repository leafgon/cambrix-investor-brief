# Founder Bio

**Sunny Park, Ph.D**

Senior Research Software Engineer at University College London and Director of LE Automaton Ltd. I build automation systems at the intersection of biology, microfluidics, and AI. My work spans full‑stack software, cloud infrastructure, embedded control, and data‑intensive pipelines, with published results in microfluidics and synthetic biology. I maintain an active academic foothold through UCL to support collaboration and validation.

**Founder Profile**

I combine deep technical leadership with hands‑on lab automation experience. I have designed and built microfluidic devices, control systems, and automation software used in peer‑reviewed studies. My career bridges software engineering, computational biology, and applied AI, with a focus on building reliable, scalable systems for data‑driven experimentation.
