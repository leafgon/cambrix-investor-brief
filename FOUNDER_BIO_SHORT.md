# Founder Bio (Short)

**Sunny Park, Ph.D** is a Senior Research Software Engineer at University College London and the Director of LE Automaton Ltd. He brings two decades of software engineering and bioengineering experience focused on lab automation, microfluidics, and data‑intensive systems.

He led the design and build of microfluidic devices, control systems, and automation software used in published studies, and has authored or coauthored peer‑reviewed work in *Nature Communications* and *Methods in Microbiology*. His scope spans full‑stack systems, cloud and DevOps, embedded and distributed systems, and applied AI/LLM integration. He maintains an active academic foothold through UCL, enabling collaboration and validation pathways.

He holds a Ph.D. in Synthetic Biology and Bioinformatics from Newcastle University and a B.Sc/B.A in Biochemistry from Columbia University.
