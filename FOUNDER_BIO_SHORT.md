# Founder Bio (Investor-Ready)

**Sunny Park, Ph.D** is a senior research software engineer at University College London and the director of LE Automaton Ltd. He combines two decades of software engineering with deep experience in synthetic biology, microfluidics, and lab automation.

He led the design and build of microfluidic devices, control systems, and automation software used in published studies, and has authored or coauthored peer‑reviewed work in *Nature Communications* and *Methods in Microbiology*. His technical scope spans full‑stack systems, cloud and DevOps, embedded and distributed systems, and applied AI/LLM integration.

He holds a Ph.D. in Synthetic Biology and Bioinformatics from Newcastle University and a B.Sc/B.A in Biochemistry from Columbia University.
